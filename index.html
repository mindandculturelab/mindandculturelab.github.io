Cooperation · Ages 2–3 · Child Development (2026)

Two- and Three-Year-Olds Coordinate Decisions in Dyads and Triads

Alissa Rivero, Jared Vasil, & Michael Tomasello

Across 40 peer groups (N = 100 children), 2- and 3-year-olds solved a pure coordination box task in pairs and triads. Interactive charts, trial-by-trial datasets, R Markdown analysis scripts, and a full 3D apparatus walkthrough are available openly.

About the Lab

Department of Psychology & Neuroscience
Duke University · Durham, NC

The Mind & Culture (MAC) Lab investigates how culture shapes the developing mind. We work with families all around the globe, bringing together tools from developmental psychology and anthropology to better understand cognitive development across cultures.

Because rigorous science should be transparent and accessible, this website brings together our open datasets, reproducible analysis scripts, study documentation, and interactive 3D walkthroughs.
