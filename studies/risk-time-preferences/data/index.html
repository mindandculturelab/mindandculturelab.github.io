Decision Making · Amir, Jordan, McAuliffe, … & Dunham (2020)

Risk & Time Preferences Across Market Contexts

400 children and adolescents across the USA, India, Indigenous Toba/Qom communities in Argentina, and two Shuar regions in Amazonian Ecuador (higher-market Upano Valley vs. lower-market Cross-Cutucú) completed 5 intertemporal choices and 5 risky marble-draw choices.
