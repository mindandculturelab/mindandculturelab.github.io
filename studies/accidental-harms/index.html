Moral Development & Cooperation

Children Are More Forgiving of Accidental Harms Across Development

Dorsa Amir

Ages 5–10

OSF repository Preprint

Research question

How does forgiveness develop across childhood, and how do a transgressor’s intent and remorse shape children’s decisions to forgive rather than punish?

Testing 5- to 10-year-olds (N = 257) in a behavioral task pitting forgiveness against punishment, this study finds high baseline forgiveness when intent and remorse are unknown, alongside age-related increases in sensitivity to intent—with older children increasingly forgiving accidental harms.
