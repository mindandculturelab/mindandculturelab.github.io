Choices & Reaction Times Across Seven Societies

808 child actors (ages 4–15; 11,931 valid reaction-time trials) played the Inequity Game across Canada, India, Maya communities in Mexico, Peru, Senegal, Uganda, and the USA, modeled computationally via the Drift-Diffusion Model (DDM).
