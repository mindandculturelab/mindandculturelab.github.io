Moral Development & Cooperation

Computational Signatures of Inequity Aversion in Children Across Seven Societies

Dorsa Amir

Ages 4–15

OSF repository

Research question

What cognitive processes underlie children’s rejections of unequal resource distributions—both when they receive less than a peer and when they receive more—across diverse societies?

Using drift-diffusion modeling of fairness decisions and reaction times across seven societies, this project characterizes the developmental and cultural dynamics of disadvantageous and advantageous inequity aversion.
