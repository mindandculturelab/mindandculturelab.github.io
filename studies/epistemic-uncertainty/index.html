Preferences for Epistemic Versus Physical Uncertainty Across Three Diverse Cultures

Dorsa Amir

Ages 5–12 and 18+

OSF repository

Research question

Do children and adults across diverse cultural environments distinguish between epistemic uncertainty (not yet knowing an already-determined outcome) and physical uncertainty (outcomes not yet determined), and do preferences between the two shift across development?

Data and analysis materials compare preferences for epistemic versus physical uncertainty across three diverse cultures to test whether uncertainty preferences vary by age or cultural context.
