Trustworthiness vs. Generosity in Children

118 children ages 5–8 pulled a 6-Starburst blue dish from a two-channel Plexiglass Trust Apparatus—either because a peer forfeited a short tool piece to help them reach it (Trustworthiness) or without peer sacrifice (Generosity).

Candies shared out of 6 by condition and age

Children returned significantly more Starburst candies when a peer had entrusted them with a tool piece to reach the 6-candy dish (M = 2.70) than in the matched Generosity control (M = 2.12, p = .002).

Actual sharing vs. perceived partner expectations

Comparison between the number of candies children actually shared (0–6) and how many candies they predicted their partner expected to receive back.
